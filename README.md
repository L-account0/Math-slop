## [The eventual cyclic minimum for unique multiset sums](The-Eventual-Cyclic-Minimum-for-Unique-Multiset-Sums-November-27-2026.pdf)

For all sufficiently large $n$, the moduli below $2^n$ admitting
a valid $n$-tag cyclic family are exactly $2^n-2^t$ for
$0\le t\le\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor$. The paper also proves
$D\ge(45/32)2^{n-1}$ for every valid family with $n\ge3$.

## [Near-Linear Riffle Rank of Identity Tensors over Commutative Rings](riffle-rank.pdf)

November 27 working draft.

The paper constructs an integral riffle decomposition of the identity tensor
that specializes to every commutative ring with identity. For a fixed number
of pairs it gives rank $n^{1+o(1)}$ over every nonzero such ring. It also
determines the fixed-alphabet exponential growth rate over the integers and
the binary field.

## [The Complexity of Three-Factor Geometric Hyperdeterminants](Three-Factor-Geometric-Hyperdeterminants.pdf)

Unified review edition.

The unified preprint proves deterministic NP-hardness for the equal-sided
geometric zero test and parsimonious hardness for counting singular triples
and measuring local multiplicity. It includes the near-boundary split-slice
results and a border circuit reduction for the reduced geometric
hyperdeterminant in sufficiently large interior formats. The quantitative
lower bound uses OpenAI's November 24 Hamiltonian-cycle theorem.

## [Deterministic Hardness of Zero Testing for Equal-Sided Geometric Hyperdeterminants](Deterministic-Hardness-of-Zero-Testing-for-Equal-Sided-Geometric-Hyperdeterminants-November-26-2026.pdf)

November 26 edition.

A deterministic polynomial-time reduction proves NP-hardness of zero
testing on equal-sided three-factor tensors with strictly positive integer
entries. It preserves the exact number of singular triples and gives
parsimonious counting hardness for the local hyperdeterminant multiplicity.

## [The Complexity of Zero Testing for Three-Factor Geometric Hyperdeterminants](The-Complexity-of-Zero-Testing-for-Three-Factor-Geometric-Hyperdeterminants-November-25-2026.pdf)

November 25 edition.

Theorem 1 proves deterministic NP-hardness of geometric hyperdeterminant zero
testing for the formats $(2d-1)\times d\times d$ and $(2d-2)\times d\times d$.
In both families the problem is NP-complete on dense integer tensors with
nonzero symmetric rank-one slices $u_i u_i^{\mathsf T}$ whose factors have
nonnegative integer coordinates. Theorem 2 gives a one-sided randomized
reduction for equal-sided tensors. The paper also gives the split-slice
factorization, counting results, and fixed-characteristic extensions.

## [Preimage Reporting with Certified Worst-Case Query Time](Preimage-Reporting-with-Certified-Worst-Case-Query-Time-November-25-2026.pdf)

The paper gives a static preimage index that reports every preimage within
the collision-sensitive worst-case query bound for every accepted index.
The construction uses isolated walks and stores uncovered inputs explicitly.
It also supports exact counting without enumerating the output.

## [From formal Semaev polynomials to the sparse permanent](Formal-Semaev-Polynomials-to-the-Sparse-Permanent-November-25-2026.pdf)

Draft for specialist review.

The draft shows how a circuit for the full formal Semaev polynomial on a
fixed elliptic curve yields a border circuit for the permanent of a bipartite
graph. The quantitative lower bound uses the sparse-support theorem in the
November 24 permanent preprint.
